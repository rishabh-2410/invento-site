Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Open Invento and register. If you are setting up a new organization, enter your business name, address, and email, then sign in with the account you created.

How do I log a stock movement?
Open the Movement tab. Choose Receive, Sale, Return, or Damage, pick a product and warehouse, enter the quantity and a reason, then confirm. Inventory totals update immediately and the entry appears in History.

How do I check current stock?
Open the Inventory tab to see stock by product. You can search the list. The Dashboard also shows current inventory, low-stock products, and out-of-stock products.

Where is my movement history?
Open the History tab for the full list, newest first. The Dashboard shows recent movements; tap View All to open History.

How do I add products, categories, or warehouses?
Sign in as an owner, go to Settings, then open Products, Categories, or Warehouses. Use the add button on that screen. New items appear in Movement pickers after the lists refresh.

How do I edit or delete a product, category, or warehouse?
Owners can open the item from Settings and use Edit or Delete. Deleting cannot be undone.

How do I add or remove employees?
Owners can open Settings, then Employees. Add an employee with their name, email, and password. Swipe an active employee to delete them.

What can employees do?
Employees can log stock movements and view inventory, history, and the dashboard for their business. Only owners can manage products, categories, warehouses, and employees.

Why did I see a session expired message?
Sign in again. Keeping the app in use helps you stay signed in; reopening the app can restore your session automatically.
